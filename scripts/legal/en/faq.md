# Frequently Asked Questions

**WENZE TII NDAKU**

---

## 1. General questions

**1.1 What is WENZE TII NDAKU?**
WENZE TII NDAKU is a Congolese digital marketplace that connects vendors and buyers on a single secure platform. You can buy beauty products, clothing, accessories, technology products and much more.

**1.2 Where does WENZE TII NDAKU operate?**
WENZE TII NDAKU operates mainly in the Democratic Republic of Congo, in Kinshasa and Lubumbashi, with a vision of pan-African and international expansion.

**1.3 Does WENZE TII NDAKU sell the products directly?**
No. WENZE TII NDAKU is an intermediary platform. Products are sold by independent vendors registered on the platform. WENZE TII NDAKU only manages the transactions on the platform and the delivery operations.

---

## 2. For buyers

**2.1 How do I create an account?**

1. Click "Sign up".
2. Enter your information (name, email address and phone number).
3. Confirm your account with a code received by email.
4. Start shopping.

**2.2 How do I place an order?**

1. Choose your product.
2. Add it to your cart.
3. Proceed to payment.
4. Confirm the order.

You will receive a confirmation by email or text message.

**2.3 Which payment methods are accepted?**
WENZE TII NDAKU accepts:
- Mobile Money
- Bank card (through a secure payment gateway)
- Payment on delivery for certain items

**2.4 Is payment secure?**
Yes. Transactions are protected by encryption systems that comply with international standards.

**2.5 How long does delivery take?**
The time depends on the vendor, the availability of the product and the delivery city. However, we try to guarantee deliveries in less than 5 days.

**2.6 Can I return a product?**
Yes, if the product is damaged, does not match the description, or if the vendor accepts returns. The conditions are specified on each product page.

---

## 3. For vendors

**3.1 How do I become a vendor on WENZE TII NDAKU?**

1. Create a vendor account.
2. Provide the required documents.
3. Wait for validation.
4. Publish your products.

**3.2 Are there fees for selling?**
WENZE TII NDAKU charges platform usage fees and a commission on monthly sales volume, or optional promotional fees. The conditions are specified in the vendor contract.

**3.3 When do I get paid?**
Payments are released after delivery confirmation and the expiry of the claim period. Payouts are made monthly according to the internal policy.

**3.4 Which products are prohibited?**
It is prohibited to sell illegal products, counterfeits, expired or dangerous products. WENZE TII NDAKU reserves the right to suspend any account in violation.

---

## 4. Security and trust

**4.1 How does WENZE TII NDAKU protect my data?**
Your personal data is protected in accordance with international digital security standards.

**4.2 What should I do if there is a problem with an order?**
Go to My Account → My Orders → Report a problem. The WENZE TII NDAKU team will analyze the situation and propose a solution.

**4.3 Can my account be suspended?**
Yes, in case of fraud, failure to comply with the general terms and conditions, or abusive behavior.
