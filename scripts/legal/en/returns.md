# Return and Refund Policy

**WENZE TII NDAKU**
Last updated: 25 February 2026

---

## 1. Preamble

This Return and Refund Policy governs the conditions that apply to products purchased on the WENZE TII NDAKU platform.

WENZE TII NDAKU acts exclusively as an intermediary platform (marketplace) connecting independent vendors and buyers. The products sold are offered and shipped by third-party vendors registered on the platform.

---

## 2. Claim period

The customer has seven (7) calendar days from the date the product is received to submit a return request through their user account.

Any request made after this period may be refused, except in justified exceptional cases.

---

## 3. Eligibility conditions

A product may be returned only in the following cases:
- Product damaged on delivery
- Product not matching the published description
- Proven manufacturing defect
- Obvious error in the order

The product must be unused, in its original condition, returned in its original packaging and accompanied by proof of purchase.

---

## 4. Non-eligible products

Unless there is a proven defect, the following products cannot be returned:
- Opened or used cosmetic products
- Underwear and intimate items
- Downloadable digital products
- Personalized or made-to-measure items
- Food products
- Items on promotion or clearance

---

## 5. Return procedure

1. Send an email to wtn-customers@outlook.com.
2. Include the registered customer's name and an identity document in the email.
3. Attach a copy of the order confirmation.
4. Describe the reason for the return clearly and in detail.
5. Attach supporting photos if necessary.

The request will be reviewed by the WENZE TII NDAKU team in collaboration with the vendor concerned.

---

## 6. Validation and refund

After the return is validated:
- The refund will be made through the original payment method where possible.
- The refund time may vary between 5 and 14 working days.

A replacement, a credit note or a partial refund may be offered depending on the case.

---

## 7. Return costs

- Paid by the vendor if the product is defective or incorrect.
- Paid by the customer if the return is due to a change of mind (if accepted).

---

## 8. Responsibility

WENZE TII NDAKU acts as a technical intermediary. Responsibility for the quality and conformity of products lies with the vendor.

WENZE TII NDAKU reserves the right to suspend a vendor in case of repeated abuse, or to refuse clearly abusive requests.

---

## 9. Disputes

In case of disagreement, WENZE TII NDAKU will carry out an internal mediation procedure.

If no agreement is reached, the dispute may be submitted to the competent courts in accordance with the laws applicable in the Democratic Republic of Congo.
